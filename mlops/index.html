Overview

What this course does

Most people finish a course knowing how to train a model in a notebook — and have no idea what happens next. This course closes that gap. You build one project and carry it end to end, so by day three you have a real model running on the internet that you can put on your CV.

Build, don't just watch

Six live labs and a capstone. Every concept is followed immediately by hands-on work, in pairs.

Industry tools, zero cost

MLflow, FastAPI, Docker, GitHub Actions, and a free host (Hugging Face Spaces / Render). No cloud bills.

Interview-ready

Assessed by a live demo and a mock technical interview — everything you build becomes your portfolio.
