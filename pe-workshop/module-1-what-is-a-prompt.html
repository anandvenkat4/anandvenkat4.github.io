What a prompt actually is

A prompt is not a question. It is a specification — and almost every "the model is bad" complaint turns out to be a specification you did not write.
Start here

"Tell me about this claim."

That prompt is grammatical, polite, and almost useless. The model has to guess what you want, what counts as relevant, how long to be, and what shape the answer should take. It will guess differently every time.

Below you can repair it one line at a time and watch the quality climb — without ever changing the model.

The context window, and the middle you should not trust

Everything the model sees — your instructions, examples, documents, the conversation — shares one window. And position matters: a fact buried in the middle of a long context is measurably more likely to be ignored.

Instructions first or lastNever bury the task in the middle of a long pasted document.
A bigger window is not a filing cabinetFitting 40 pages in does not mean all 40 are used. Retrieve what matters instead — that is RAG, in M6a.
Non-English costs moreThe same sentence in Tamil or Hindi uses noticeably more tokens than in English.
The idea to carry forward

Nothing in this module required a better model

Every gain came from being explicit — task, criteria, boundaries, format — and from choosing the right temperature. Reach for clarity before you reach for anything clever. The rest of the course is what to do when clarity genuinely is not enough.
